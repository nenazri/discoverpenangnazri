Project Introduction

Discover Penang is a tourism and heritage website developed to introduce visitors to the historical attractions and cultural heritage of Penang, Malaysia.

Project Objectives

The website provides information about Penang’s historical attractions and introduces its rich cultural heritage. It also features a ticket cost calculator to help visitors estimate their expenses, along with registration and login functions to demonstrate the visitor account system.
