Historical Attractions in Penang

Discover the history and heritage of Penang.

Penang Street Art

Penang street art is a collection of murals, interactive installations, and steel-rod sculptures scattered across George Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Penang, Malaysia.

Bukit Bendera

Penang Hill was developed during the British colonial era as a cool retreat from the tropical heat. Its funicular railway opened in 1923, making it one of Penang's most iconic historical attractions.

Fort Cornwallis

Explore a historical fort associated with Penang's colonial history.

Penang War Museum

The Penang War Museum is Southeast Asia's largest war museum, set on a 20-acre hillside in Batu Maung, Penang, on the site of a former 1930s British defense fortress.

P.Ramlee Herritage House

P. Ramlee House is a heritage museum and preserved traditional wooden kampung house on stilts celebrating the life and legacy of iconic Malaysian entertainer P. Ramlee.
